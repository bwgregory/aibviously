OAuth helper for @aibviously

This GitHub Pages site provides the redirect URI and policy pages required by TikTok and Meta developer apps used for @aibviously / vid-producer social posting.

What this site does

It does not log you in or store tokens. It only hosts a safe OAuth callback landing page and the public Privacy Policy and Terms of Service links that developer consoles require.
